# Majom vs Notion

Notion is a document and database-first workspace. Majom is a visual planning workspace for individuals.

## Main Difference

Notion is strongest when the main unit of work is a page, database, or structured document. Majom is strongest when the main need is visual reasoning across goals, tasks, boards, routines, and ideas.

## When Notion Fits Better

Notion fits better for documentation, databases, wikis, and structured knowledge management.

## When Majom Fits Better

Majom fits better when an individual wants to plan visually, connect work items spatially, and move from ideas to tasks without losing the big picture.

## Summary

Choose Notion for documents and databases. Choose Majom for personal visual planning with connected execution.
