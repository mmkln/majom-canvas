# Privacy And Personal Workspace

Majom is positioned as a personal workspace for individual planning.

The product is designed around one person's goals, tasks, routines, boards, ideas, and planning context. This makes privacy and personal ownership important parts of the product direction.

## Personal Rather Than Team-First

Majom is not currently positioned as a team-first project management platform. The core experience is built around individual clarity and private planning.

## Why Privacy Matters For Planning

Personal planning often includes unfinished ideas, sensitive goals, uncertain priorities, and private notes. A planning workspace should make it easy to think clearly without forcing team process or public collaboration.

## Product Direction

Majom should continue to communicate clearly when data is private, how AI assistance is used, and how the product supports individual ownership of plans.
