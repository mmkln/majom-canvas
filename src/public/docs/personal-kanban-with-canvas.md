# Personal Kanban With Canvas Context

Personal kanban helps individuals track work across stages such as backlog, in progress, and done. Majom extends this idea by connecting board execution back to a visual planning canvas.

Instead of treating a board as an isolated list of cards, Majom keeps tasks and boards close to goals, routines, and broader planning context.

## Why Combine Kanban And Canvas?

A board is good for execution. A canvas is good for understanding relationships, dependencies, and the larger picture.

Majom uses both patterns so an individual can move from thinking to execution without losing context.

## Use Cases

- Planning a product idea from goal to tasks
- Managing independent work without a team process
- Turning research notes into project actions
- Keeping routines and priorities visible beside execution work

## Difference From Team Boards

Majom is not built around team coordination, assignments, or organizational reporting. Its board experience is intended for personal execution and individual clarity.
