# AI Planning Workspace

Majom is an AI-assisted planning workspace for individuals. AI support is intended to help a person clarify goals, break down work, organize thoughts, and improve planning structure.

The AI layer is not meant to replace personal judgment. It supports the planning process while the user remains the owner of the plan.

## How AI Fits Into Majom

- Clarifying vague goals
- Decomposing large work into smaller steps
- Turning notes into structured tasks
- Suggesting relationships between ideas
- Helping refine personal planning systems

## Individual-First AI Assistance

Majom is designed for individual planning. AI assistance should help one person think more clearly, not add team process overhead.

## Related Capabilities

Majom combines AI-assisted planning with canvas thinking, personal boards, routines, goals, and tasks.
