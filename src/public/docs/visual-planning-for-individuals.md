# Visual Planning For Individuals

Visual planning is the practice of mapping goals, ideas, tasks, and relationships before reducing them into a list.

Majom uses visual planning to help individuals see how their work connects. A canvas can hold goals, project areas, dependent tasks, routines, and loose thoughts in one place.

## Why Visual Planning Matters

Many planning tools begin with lists, boards, or documents. Those formats are useful once work is already structured, but individual planning often starts earlier: with uncertainty, scattered notes, vague intentions, and relationships that are hard to see in a list.

Majom gives that early thinking a visual surface.

## How Majom Supports Visual Planning

- Canvas-first planning for goals and ideas
- Connections between planning elements
- Boards for execution after the plan becomes concrete
- Routines for recurring personal work
- AI assistance for clarifying and decomposing plans

## Best Fit

Majom is a good fit for people who think spatially, plan across several life or work areas, and want to keep strategy, tasks, and routines connected in one personal workspace.
