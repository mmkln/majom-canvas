# What Is Majom?

Majom is a visual planning workspace for individuals. It helps one person connect goals, tasks, boards, routines, and ideas in a single private workspace.

Majom is designed for solo planning rather than team-first project management. The product combines a visual canvas, personal task structure, boards, routines, and AI-assisted thinking so an individual can turn scattered work into a clearer system.

## Who Majom Is For

Majom is for individual makers, creators, students, independent builders, and solo operators who need a personal planning system.

It is useful when work starts as loose ideas, goals, research notes, or unfinished plans and needs to become concrete next actions.

## What Majom Helps With

- Visual planning on a canvas
- Breaking goals into tasks and next actions
- Keeping personal boards connected to broader plans
- Tracking routines and focus loops
- Using AI to clarify and structure thinking

## What Majom Is Not

Majom is not positioned as a team-first project management platform. It is not primarily a company workflow tool, a shared whiteboard, or a document database.

The primary focus is individual clarity: helping one person reason through work and maintain a private operating system for planning.
